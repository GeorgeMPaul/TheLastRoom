# 🔍 THE LAST ROOM
### A Mystery Puzzle Game — Revised Full Storyline & Scene Design

---

## THE PREMISE

**The Last Room** is an isometric mystery set entirely within one space: a **studio apartment on the 4th floor of Elara Residences, HSR Layout, Bangalore.** Unit 4B. It belongs to **MIRA SEN**, 23 — an indie musician and part-time UX freelancer who moved to Bangalore from Kolkata two years ago to "make it on her own terms."

The apartment is small but deeply personal. Fairy lights strung above the bed. Movie posters blu-tacked at slightly wrong angles. A guitar with something scrawled on it in marker. A vintage Marshall speaker that cost more than her month's rent. It is the room of someone who is still figuring out who they are, and doing it joyfully.

On Monday, March 18, 2024, her neighbor calls the building manager when he notices her morning Spotify station — always audible through the thin walls — hasn't played since Sunday night.

They find Mira in her bed. Still. Pale. Gone.

## THE CHARACTERS

The player builds their understanding of each character across all scenes. Here is who they are:

---

**MIRA SEN** — The victim. 23. She moved to Bangalore with a secondhand guitar and a Notion board full of plans. She had 12,000 Instagram followers who loved her bedroom covers, a freelance UX income that barely kept her afloat, and an EP she'd been recording for eight months. She was funny, scattered, and more perceptive than she ever let on. She wrote notes to herself on sticky paper, kept a solved Rubik's cube she'd never touched again ("proof I did it once"), and texted in lowercase.

**KABIR ROY** — 25. Mira's ex-boyfriend of two years, broken up three months ago. Also a musician, but one who peaked at college fest performances and never moved past it. He introduced Mira to the Bangalore music scene, then watched her outgrow him within a year of arriving. He says the breakup was mutual. Everyone who knows them knows it wasn't. He still had a key to her apartment — she kept meaning to ask for it back.

**TANYA SHARMA** — 24. Mira's best friend from NIFT Bangalore. Now a marketing executive at a D2C startup. She is the loudest person in any room and the most reliably fun. She introduced Mira to Veer Malhotra when Mira's music started gaining traction. Eight months ago, she lent Mira ₹80,000 for rent and equipment. Mira recently consulted a lawyer about whether it could be classified legally as a gift. Tanya doesn't know that Mira knows she knows.

**VEER MALHOTRA** — 31. Music producer. Runs an indie label called *Hollow Mountain Records.* He signed Mira six months ago after finding her YouTube covers. The contract, which Mira signed without a lawyer, gives Hollow Mountain full ownership of her first EP — including posthumous rights. If she dies before the release, the label keeps everything. He is smooth, well-dressed, and says all the right things in exactly the right order.

**NEHA SEN** — 28. Mira's older sister. Lives in Pune with her husband. Practical, responsible, and quietly exhausted by Mira's choices. She co-holds a joint savings account left by their late parents — approximately ₹4.2 lakh — which Mira controls because she was the younger child and their parents wanted her to have stability. Neha has been asking for a formal split of the account for fourteen months. Two months ago their conversation about this turned into their worst fight. They hadn't spoken since.

**ROHAN MEHTA** — 24. Mira's next-door neighbor, Unit 4A. Software engineer at a mid-size SaaS company. Quiet, helpful, always there. He fixed her WiFi, assembled her IKEA chair, and accepted every request to hold packages with zero complaints. He was in love with her. She knew and chose not to address it because she valued having him around. His Spotify Wrapped 2023 was 90% the same artists as hers. He knew her schedule better than anyone in the building.

## THE ROOM — ISOMETRIC LAYOUT

The apartment is a single studio with the following permanent zones:

- **The Bed Zone** — Against the south wall. Iron bed frame with fairy lights above. Pillow hug toy always on the left side. Curtains flanking the window above.
- **The Work Corner** — East wall. Desk with desktop computer, keyboard, mouse, study lamp. Chair pulled out at a slight angle (she never pushed it in). Books stacked beside it.
- **The Music Corner** — West wall. Vintage Marshall speaker. Guitar on a stand or leaning. A small cushion on the floor for sitting cross-legged.
- **The Window** — North wall, large. The city view. The only source of natural light.
- **The Mirror** — On the wall beside the bathroom door. Full-length. She used it to check outfits and record phone videos.
- **The Rug** — Center of the room. Colorful, slightly askew. Her version of a living room.
- **Shelves** — Floating shelves above the desk. Books, globe, flower pot, small objects.
- **Entryway** — A small ledge as you walk in. She dropped everything there — pouches, keys, plates of food she carried from the kitchen.

## LEVEL 1 — THE CRIME SCENE
**Task: WHERE — Was Mira murdered here, in this room? Or was her body moved?**
**Date: Monday, March 18, 2024 | 9:15 AM | Overcast, flat grey light. No shadows.**

---

**Scene Description:**

The room is undisturbed — and that is the first disturbing thing. Nothing is knocked over. Nothing is broken. Mira lies in her bed in a loose t-shirt, on her back, one arm curled around her pillow hug toy. She looks asleep. Her phone is on the chair beside the bed (acting as a nightstand), face-down. The study lamp is off. The curtains are half-drawn. The fairy lights above the bed are still on — they run on a timer, 6 PM to 6 AM, but it's 9 AM now. *The timer failed, or was unplugged and manually switched back on.*

---

**Assets Present:**
`bed` `window` `curtains` `mirror` `rug` `chair` `pillow hug toy` `cup and saucer` `books` `portraits` `movie posters` `guitar` `vintage marshall speaker` `plant` `flower pot` `small cushion` `study lamp (off)` `laptop (open on rug)`

---

**INVESTIGATION CLUES:**

**① The Cup and Saucer (on the chair beside the bed):**
A mug of something — chamomile, or something honey-colored. At the very bottom, a faint powdery film coats the inside of the cup. The saucer has a faint ring from a second cup — but there is no second cup in the room. *Someone else was drinking here and took their cup with them when they left.*

**② The Rug (center of room):**
Two distinct impressions in the rug's pile — both facing the bed, close together. Two people sat on this rug for some time. Mira's laptop is here, lid open, screen black (battery dead). But there are two small cushion dents. *Two people were sitting on the rug together, laptops or phones out, before Mira went to bed.*

**③ The Guitar:**
On the stand, in the music corner. The capo is clipped on the 5th fret. In every photo and video on Mira's Instagram, the capo is always on the 2nd fret — her default for her own songs. Someone else played it, or adjusted it, and left it that way. *The capo placement is someone else's preference.*

**④ The Window:**
Closed. Latched from the inside. No signs of forced entry on the latch or the frame. The only entry and exit is the main door. *No one came in or out through the window. This was not a break-in.*

**⑤ The Mirror:**
A small lipstick drawing on the lower corner — a tiny heart, the kind you do absentmindedly. The shade is a burnt coral-orange. Mira wears browns and nudes, based on every photo in the room. *The lipstick color belongs to someone who visited.*

**⑥ The Fairy Lights:**
Still on at 9 AM. The timer plug is visibly *unplugged* from the extension board and the lights are connected directly. Someone physically overrode the timer to leave the lights on — either Mira did it for comfort before she went to sleep, or someone else did it after she was already unconscious. *A gesture. Small. Telling.*

---

**CHARACTER CLUES:**

**① Portraits on the wall:**
A pinboard of photos introduces the player to the characters visually for the first time. Mira and a boy (laughing, arms around each other, a stage behind them) — *this is Kabir.* Mira and a girl in a rooftop setting, both slightly drunk, mid-cackle — *Tanya.* Mira in a recording studio with a well-dressed older man, both looking pleased — *Veer.* A family photo: Mira, a slightly older, neater woman, and two elderly people — *Neha, and their late parents.*

**② Movie Posters:**
*Whiplash* (Fletcher's "Are you rushing or are you dragging?" quote visible) and *A Star is Born.* Both stories of musicians who burn bright and destroy themselves reaching for something. *She knew what she was aiming for and had made peace with the cost.* It tells us who Mira was.

**③ Books:**
Three visible titles. *"So You Want to Talk About Money"* by Erin Lowry (a bookmark 60% in). *"This Is Marketing"* by Seth Godin (spine cracked, annotated). And a dog-eared copy of *"Maybe You Should Talk to Someone."* She was working on her finances, her career, and her mental health — all simultaneously, all urgently.

**④ The Guitar:**
Written on the body of the guitar in black marker, in Mira's handwriting: *"KABIR'S HANDS OFF THIS."* Playful, possessive, past-tense in the worst way. He was the kind of person you'd write warnings about even while smiling.

**⑤ Plant and Flower Pot:**
The plant is thriving and recently watered (moist soil). The flower pot has a small sticky note attached to it: *"water me every 3 days or i will haunt you — Mira."* She left notes to herself everywhere. It tells you she was alive and planning ahead as recently as a few days ago.

---

**DEDUCTION REQUIRED:**
The player must confirm: no forced entry, no signs of a struggle, two people in the room (rug impressions, missing second cup), Mira went to bed willingly. This rules out a violent attack from a stranger. **She was with someone she trusted, consumed something she didn't know was contaminated, and went to sleep and didn't wake up.** The room is the primary scene. Her body was not moved.

**Answer: Yes. The Last Room itself — Unit 4B, Elara Residences. Mira was murdered exactly where she was found.**

## LEVEL 2 — TWO NIGHTS BEFORE
**Task: WEAPON — How was Mira killed?**
**Date: Saturday, March 16, 2024 | 11:00 PM | Night. Warm indoor light. The city hums outside.**

---

**Scene Description:**

The room is alive. Warm lamp glow. The vintage Marshall playing something lo-fi and low. The rug has two people's worth of chaos on it — a laptop open, a half-eaten donut on a plate, two cups in use. Mira is here, cross-legged on the floor, laughing at something on her screen. A visitor is beside her. (The visitor is rendered as a silhouette — the player knows someone is there but cannot yet identify them.) The room has the comfortable disorder of a close friendship.

---

**Assets Present:**
`bed (unmade, used as backstop)` `window (closed, night)` `curtains (closed)` `mirror` `rug` `chair` `guitar (leaning against wall)` `vintage marshall speaker` `cup and saucer (×2)` `plate` `donut` `laptop` `pillow hug toy` `small cushion` `movie posters` `portraits` `plant` `books` `speaker system`

**NOT PRESENT** (logic): *desktop computer* (she moved it to make floor space), *study lamp* (off, only lamp is on), *bunch of files, mouse, keyboard* (cleared away for the hangout), *globe, flower pot, snail toy, rubix cube, pouch* (not contextually present this night)

---

**INVESTIGATION CLUES:**

**① The Two Cups:**
Both cups on the rug — one is Mira's (blue enamel, her usual) and one is a white ceramic mug from her kitchen. The white mug is closer to where the visitor sat. *The visitor used the white mug.* But here is the crucial detail: clicking on the blue enamel cup shows a faint pale powder residue along the inside rim — not at the bottom where tea dregs settle, but near the top. *Something was added to Mira's cup while it already had liquid in it — dropped in from above, dissolving quickly.*

**② The Donut on the Plate:**
Mira's dietary quirk, established by her Instagram food posts — she always eats donuts from the bottom up ("the frosting hits different as the finale"). The donut on the plate has been eaten from the top. *Mira didn't eat this donut. The visitor did, and left the plate.*

**③ The Laptop (Open, On Rug):**
Screen shows a YouTube tab — Mira's own channel, playing one of her covers. Below it in the browser: a Gmail tab, minimized. Clicking the Gmail: a draft, unsent, to *veer@hollowmountain.in*, subject line: *"Question about the contract — urgent."* Body: *"Veer, I spoke to someone about the EP deal and I think I need a proper lawyer to review clause 7b before the release date. Can we talk?"* *She was about to challenge her music contract. This email was never sent.*

**④ The Vintage Marshall Speaker:**
A faint sticky note on the back: *"a gift from K — don't let him take it back."* Kabir gave her this. It's her most expensive possession. The fact that it's labeled suggests she was prepared for him to ask for it. *He resented giving it. She knew.*

**⑤ The Guitar (Leaning Against the Wall):**
The capo is now on the 2nd fret — Mira's position. Between Level 1 and this scene, the player is traveling backwards in time. The capo moved from the 5th fret (crime scene) to the 2nd (this night). *Someone changed the capo after Saturday night. The visitor adjusted it.*

**⑥ The Speaker System (On, Playing):**
The playlist on the laptop synced to the speaker is visible: Phoebe Bridgers, Mitski, Raveena, Novo Amor. At the bottom of the queue, someone queued a song that doesn't fit: *"Murder on the Dancefloor"* by Sophie Ellis-Bextor. *A joke? Or something that will feel less funny later.*

---

**CHARACTER CLUES:**

**① The Visitor (Silhouette):**
The player cannot identify them yet, but two details are visible: *a bright coral-orange nail polish* on the visitor's hands (matches the lipstick heart in the mirror from Level 1), and a *tote bag near the door* with the logo of a D2C startup: *"Briq."* The player will file this away.

**② Portraits — New Detail:**
One photo has a sticky note over it — placed recently, covering Kabir's face in the photo of him and Mira on stage: *"processing 🙃"* in Mira's lowercase. She was still working through the breakup.

**③ The "Money" Book:**
The bookmark has moved further in since Level 1 — she was reading it faster recently. A folded piece of paper is tucked inside: a screenshot printout of a WhatsApp conversation. Partially visible: *"—you know that was a loan right mira" / "tanya i literally asked you at the time"* / "I have the texts—"* The rest is obscured. *A dispute, documented.*

**④ Pillow Hug Toy:**
Tossed on the bed beside her guitar jacket. It has a small fabric patch on it — shaped like a musical note — that wasn't factory-original. Mira sewed it on herself. She kept childhood things and made them more *her.* She was still becoming someone. This is what makes the scene ache.

---

**DEDUCTION REQUIRED:**
The powder residue in Mira's cup, added from above while she was looking at the screen. The visitor used their own clean cup. Mira was poisoned through her drink — something dissolved into her tea while she was distracted. The weapon is not physical. It is pharmaceutical.

**Answer: Poisoning. Something dissolved into Mira's drink while she was looking away. Chemical, careful, and designed to look like she went to sleep.**

## DESIGN NOTES

**Asset Logic Across Scenes:**
The guitar's capo position is the game's most elegant continuity thread — moving from crime scene (5th fret, wrong) backwards through scenes to Valentine's evening (2nd fret, right), teaching the player that the crime scene has been *touched after the fact* before they know why. The Rubik's cube's solve-state across scenes tracks Mira's mental state arc: unsolved in December, half-done in March, complete by February — *she was at her most together right before everything collapsed.* The two cups are the game's central forensic image, appearing in Levels 1, 2, 4, 5, and 6 each time with slightly more context.

**Environmental Progression:**
Each scene has a distinct atmospheric register — flat grey morning (crime scene), warm artificial night (Saturday hangout), sharp sunny afternoon (work mode), amber dusk (Valentine's), low rainy afternoon (December), golden city-lit night (last night). The same room reads completely differently in each lighting condition, making the time travel feel genuinely disorienting and then orienting.

**Character Pacing:**
The player first sees the characters as photos on a pinboard. Then as names on cups and notes. Then as voices in messages. Then as physical presences (silhouettes, tote bags, nail polish). By Level 6, they know every person well enough to feel the reveal as a loss — not a gotcha. Tanya was their friend too, by then.

**The Emotional Design of the Killer:**
Tanya is not a monster. She is a person who felt left behind by someone she helped build up, who made one decision that was catastrophic and dressed it in ten small justifications first. The player will have liked her — her donut visits, her warm texts, her cackle in the rooftop photo. The final reveal should not feel like satisfaction. It should feel like *oh.* That specific, sinking, particular oh.

*That is the game's real final question: not who did it, but how do you hold two true things at once — that she was your best friend, and that she chose this.*
